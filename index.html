How to use this scaffold

Start with a habit, then attach the artifact.

The site is organized around repeatable PM moments: choosing what matters, learning from customers, aligning teams, launching responsibly, and measuring outcomes. Templates and agents are intentionally lightweight so your team can adapt them without fighting the format.

Good Practice Guide

Product habits that keep work grounded.

01

Frame the problem before the solution

Write the user, pain, context, evidence, business impact, and constraints before discussing features.

02

Define outcomes in measurable terms

Prefer behavior and business metrics over delivery-only milestones. Every bet needs a learning signal.

03

Separate confidence from ambition

High-value ideas can still be low-confidence. Mark assumptions clearly and plan the smallest useful test.

04

Make tradeoffs visible

Show why something is in, out, or delayed. Decision logs reduce churn and improve stakeholder trust.

05

Design launches as learning systems

Launch with audience, rollout, telemetry, support readiness, risks, and rollback paths already named.

06

Review outcomes honestly

Close the loop with adoption, quality, revenue, customer feedback, and what the team will change next.

Operating Cadence

        
A simple rhythm for PM excellence.

      

      

        

          Weekly
          
Outcome review

          
Check metric movement, risks, blocked decisions, and whether current work still maps to the goal.

        

        

          Biweekly
          
Customer learning review

          
Share themes from interviews, tickets, sales calls, analytics, and competitive signals.

        

        

          Monthly
          
Roadmap confidence review

          
Re-score opportunities using impact, confidence, effort, strategy fit, and capacity.

        

        

          Quarterly
          
Strategy narrative refresh

          
Update market context, customer needs, bets, sequencing, and explicit non-goals.
